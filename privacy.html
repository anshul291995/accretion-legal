Privacy Policy

Accretion: Cosmic Merge · Effective October 20, 2026

Accretion: Cosmic Merge ("the game") is designed to work without collecting any personal information. This policy explains exactly what happens to your data.

Data we collect

None. The game has no accounts, advertising, analytics, crash reporting or tracking of any kind, and it does not send any information over the network.

Data stored on your device

  • Your best score, the celestial bodies you have discovered, the number of games played, and your sound and vibration settings are saved locally on your device so the game remembers them.
  • This data never leaves your device. It is deleted when you uninstall the game (or clear the site data in the web version).

Sharing

If you tap Share, the game opens your device's standard share sheet with a short message about your score. Nothing is sent unless you choose an app and send it yourself, and we never receive it.

Children

The game is suitable for all ages. Because it collects no personal information from anyone, it collects none from children under 13 either.

Third parties

The game contains no third-party SDKs that collect data. App stores (Apple App Store and Google Play) may collect information under their own privacy policies when you download the game.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
