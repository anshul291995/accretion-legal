How to play

  • Touch anywhere and drag to choose where on the orbit ring the next body launches from.
  • Release to drop it toward the core. Gravity pulls everything inward.
  • Two matching bodies merge into the next one: Stardust → Pebble → Comet → Asteroid → Moon → Red Planet → Ocean World → Ringed Planet → Gas Giant → Star → Black Hole.
  • Merges that happen in quick succession build a chain multiplier.
  • Two Stars collapse into a Black Hole, which devours nearby bodies for big points and frees up space.
  • If bodies stay outside the dashed Roche limit for too long, the orbit collapses and the run ends.

Common questions

  • Where is my progress saved? On your device only. Uninstalling the game removes it.
  • How do I turn off sound or vibration? Use the toggles on the title screen or in the pause menu.
  • Does the game need an internet connection? No, it works completely offline.
